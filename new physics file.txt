Core Physics: Waves transfer energy through oscillating mediums.

Transverse waves: Particle oscillations are perpendicular (

Option Breakdown: Sound waves in air propagate strictly through longitudinal pressure variations. Light waves and string waves are transverse. Sound waves in solids have both longitudinal and transverse components, but sound in air is purely longitudinal.
